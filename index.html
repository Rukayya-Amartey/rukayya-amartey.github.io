Driver Performance & Safety Analysis

A logistics operations database with 14 data sheets needed to answer a specific question: which drivers perform best, and why. I selected 3 core datasets (driver profiles, monthly performance metrics, safety incidents), built 5 PivotTables and PivotCharts in Excel, created a scatter plot linking experience to on-time delivery using AVERAGEIF formulas, and added 4 interactive slicers so the dashboard could be filtered dynamically by month, trips, and incident type.

TradeZone E-Commerce Analysis

A full performance review of TradeZone's 2023–2024 operations. After cleaning the raw data (fixing inconsistent city names, flagging duplicate customer emails, resolving order-total mismatches), I wrote 8 SQL queries covering customer acquisition, product performance, seller efficiency, and revenue trends — then summarized the findings in a memo to the Head of Growth and Head of Seller Operations. Key finding: despite 1,395% revenue growth in Q1 2024, just 603 high-spending customers were generating the vast majority of revenue, while 30-day conversion on new sign-ups stayed below 50% in every state.
